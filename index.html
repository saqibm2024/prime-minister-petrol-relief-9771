What Is the Prime Minister Special Relief Scheme?

The Prime Minister Special Relief Scheme has been introduced to provide support to citizens affected by higher petrol prices.

According to the provided scheme information, rising global oil prices and disruptions in oil supplies have increased the pressure on consumers. The government has therefore introduced a targeted petrol relief system for eligible vehicle users.

The scheme focuses on people who use motorcycles, three-wheelers, rickshaws, and small cars for their daily travel, work, education, and other important activities.

Eligible citizens can complete registration through SMS. After successful registration, they can obtain a token and use it at a petrol pump to receive petrol at a reduced cost.

How Much Petrol Relief Will You Get?

The provided scheme information states that eligible citizens can receive Rs. 100 petrol relief per liter.

The allocated petrol quota must be used in one transaction according to the provided scheme information.

Prime Minister Petrol Relief Scheme Eligibility

Applicants should check the eligibility requirements before sending the registration SMS.

There are different rules for motorcycles, three-wheelers, rickshaws, and cars.

Eligibility for Motorcycles and Three-Wheelers

Motorcycles and eligible three-wheel vehicles can qualify for the petrol relief scheme if they were registered on or after January 1, 2011.

One important condition is that the motorcycle or three-wheeler does not have to be registered in the applicant's own name.

Applicants still need to provide accurate vehicle registration information during the registration process.

Eligibility for Cars Up to 800cc

Cars with an engine capacity of 800cc or less can qualify for the scheme.

The vehicle must have been registered on or after January 1, 2006.

There is also an ownership condition for cars. The vehicle must be registered in the applicant's own name.

Therefore, applicants should make sure that their CNIC and vehicle ownership information matches the required records.

Mobile Number Requirement

The mobile number is an important part of the registration process.

The applicant must use a mobile number that is registered against their own CNIC.

You should not use a SIM registered in another person's name for your application.

How to Register for Petrol Relief Through 9771

The Prime Minister Petrol Relief Scheme registration is completed through SMS.

Applicants need to open the Messages application on their mobile phone and create a new SMS.

The registration message must contain:

  • REG
  • 13-digit CNIC number
  • Vehicle registration number
  • Province code
  • Vehicle registration date

Each piece of information should be separated by a space.

9771 Petrol Relief Registration SMS Format

REG <CNIC> <Plate No> <Province Code> <Registration Date>

Example:

REG 1234512345671 ABC-123 P 02092026

The example is only for understanding the format. Replace the example information with your own correct details.

After preparing the SMS, send it to:

9771

An SMS charge of Rs. 1.25 excluding applicable taxes is stated in the provided scheme information.

Province Codes for 9771 Registration

How to Enter the Vehicle Registration Date

The vehicle registration date must be entered in DDMMYYYY format.

  • DD = Day
  • MM = Month
  • YYYY = Year

For example:

September 2, 2026

✅ Correct: 02092026
❌ Incorrect: 02SEP2026

Do not use letters for the month. Always enter the registration date using eight numbers.

What Happens After Sending REG to 9771?

After you send the registration SMS to 9771, your information is processed for eligibility and registration.

The provided information states that electronic government records, including NADRA and Excise databases, may be used for verification.

If your registration is successful, you should receive a confirmation message.

Keep the confirmation SMS safely because you may need the information later when obtaining your petrol relief token.

If your registration fails, check your CNIC, vehicle number, province code, registration date, and mobile number before trying again.

How to Get the 9771 Petrol Relief Token

Registration is the first step. After successful registration, an eligible applicant needs to obtain a token before going to the petrol pump.

The token can also be requested through SMS.

Open your mobile Messages application and type:

TOK

Send the message to:

9771

After the request is processed, you should receive your petrol relief token through SMS.

How Long Is the 9771 Token Valid?

According to the provided scheme information, the token can be used during the next 10 days.

After the token expires, a new token may need to be requested according to the applicable scheme rules.

Applicants should use the token within its validity period and keep the token SMS on their mobile phone until the petrol transaction has been completed.

5 Liters of Petrol

800cc Car Petrol Quota

Eligible cars with an engine capacity of up to 800cc can receive:

10 Liters of Petrol

How to Get Subsidized Petrol at the Petrol Pump

After receiving your token, visit a petrol pump participating in the scheme.

Show your 10-digit token number to the petrol pump representative.

The representative will verify the token and process the eligible petrol transaction.

According to the provided information, eligible citizens can receive Rs. 100 per liter relief.

The allocated quota must be used in one transaction.

What Information Is Included in the Token SMS?

The petrol relief token SMS may contain:

  • Token number
  • Vehicle registration number
  • Petrol quota
  • Token expiry date
  • Petrol relief scheme information

Always check the information in the SMS before visiting the petrol pump.

Is Re-Registration Required?

The scheme information describes registration as a one-time registration process for eligible applicants.

After completing registration, eligible applicants can request tokens according to the applicable rules.

To request a token, send TOK to 9771.

Applicants should follow any updated government instructions if the terms or process of the scheme are changed.

Can Someone Else Use My Petrol Token?

The petrol relief token is connected with the registration and vehicle information stored in the system.

Applicants should avoid sharing their token with other people.

The token should be used for the eligible vehicle associated with the registered application.

Government Verification System

The scheme includes an electronic verification process.

According to the provided information, relevant government databases, including NADRA and Excise records, can be used to verify applicants and vehicle information.

The purpose of this verification is to make the petrol relief process more transparent and reduce the chances of duplicate or false claims.

Important Points Before Sending the 9771 SMS

Before sending your registration SMS, carefully check all the information.

  • Your mobile number is registered against your own CNIC.
  • Your CNIC contains 13 digits.
  • Your vehicle registration number is correct.
  • You have entered the correct province code.
  • Your vehicle registration date is correct.
  • The registration date is written in DDMMYYYY format.
  • Your motorcycle or three-wheeler meets the required registration date.
  • Your car is 800cc or below if applying under the car category.
  • Your car is registered in your own name.
  • You have used REG correctly for registration.
  • You have used TOK correctly when requesting a token.
